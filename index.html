3. Government Licenses & Registrations (Estimated Costs Included)

Packaged drinking water distribution requires explicit statutory certifications before starting mass-scale commercial roll-outs:

  • BIS Certification (Bureau of Indian Standards): Mandatory under IS 14543. Includes complete independent source water chemical matrix checks and plant audit validation. (Cost: ~₹2.5 Lakhs to ₹4 Lakhs including initial laboratory sampling fee)
  • FSSAI Central License: Food Safety and Standards Authority of India authorization for commercial beverage distribution. (Cost: ~₹7,500/year)
  • APPCB NOC: State Pollution Control Board Consent to Establish (CTE) and Consent to Operate (CTO). (Cost: ~₹1.5 Lakhs based on capital investment tier)
  • Central Ground Water Authority (CGWA) NOC: Mandatory permission clearance to extract sub-surface water via borewells. (Cost: ~₹1 Lakh)
  • Factories License & Fire Department NOC: Basic industrial safety conformance clearings. (Cost: ~₹2 Lakhs)
  • GST Registration & MSME Udyam Certification: Zero direct processing cost / Minimal filing administrative charges

5. Water Sourcing Cost Analysis & Ionization Workflow Matrix

Source Processing Unit Cost:

  • Borewell / Ground Extraction: Lowest extraction profile. Basic power delivery overhead. Cost matrix maps out to ₹0.15 to ₹0.20 per Litre.
  • Atmospheric Water Generation (AWG) / Air Humidity: Requires substantial electrical pull per condensate cycle. When powered entirely by our zero-grid solar-wind hybrid unit, direct external operational fuel metrics stay low, but asset depreciation assigns an internal valuation cost of ₹1.80 to ₹2.50 per Litre.
  • Blended Formula Core Target: By dynamically balancing structural delivery across a targeted triple blend ratio (e.g., 60% Pure Surface/Ground + 40% Deep Moisture Air Capture), the aggregate raw input treatment valuation locks in firmly at an optimal average of ₹0.85 per Litre.

Ionization Retention Rules:

Critical Operational Process Rule: Water should not be stored statically in raw tanks for extended hours after the ionization phase. Once copper (Cu2+) and silver (Ag+) particles are introduced via electrolysis inside the Dual-Ion Shield reactor, the water must proceed immediately to the automated inline filling track. The transformation cycle locks inside the airtight finalized bottle or cup packaging. This preserves optimal alkaline tracking (pH 8.5+) and prevents structural oxidation or potency drop before consumer intake. Raw source storage is limited to raw/filtered buffer tanks prior to ionization.
